Transform the raw discovery call notes and extracted Brand Voice Guide into an executive-ready Campaign Creative Brief.

[CONTEXT]
Client Name: [CLIENT_NAME]
Campaign Objective: [CAMPAIGN_OBJECTIVE]
Target Channel(s): [TARGET_CHANNELS]

Brand Voice Guide (from P01):
"""
[P01_BRAND_VOICE_OUTPUT]
"""

Raw Discovery Call Notes:
"""

"""

[CONSTRAINTS]
1. Organize output into clear, structured markdown headers.
2. Focus strictly on actionable directions for creative and paid media teams.
3. Exclude conversational filler or meta-commentary.
4. Keep output under 400 words.

[OUTPUT FORMAT]
1. Campaign Overview & Primary KPI
2. Core Target Audience & Key Insight
3. Creative Deliverables & Channel Matrix

## 🏢 Intended Workflow or Task

Converts unstructured kickoff call transcripts and brand traits into a single campaign creative brief to guide creative teams.

- **Trigger:** Discovery call completed and transcript processed.
- **Actor:** Lead Strategist reviews and approves.
- **Timing:** Day 1 of client onboarding (following P01 execution).
- **Next step:** Day 1 of client onboarding (following P01 execution).

```
[P01 Output] + Discovery Call Notes → [THIS PROMPT RUNS] → Approved Brief → Feeds P03 & P04
```

---

## ❗ Problem Being Solved

Drafting creative briefs from scratch takes strategists 2 to 3 hours per campaign. Notes from discovery calls are often messy, leading to missed client requirements, scope creep, and misalignment between strategy and creative execution teams.

**Pain points addressed:**
- Significant time lost organizing unstructured meeting transcripts (~2.5 hrs per brief).
- Creative briefs lacking clear KPIs or channel-specific deliverable breakdowns.
- Miscommunication between account management and internal design/copy teams

## ⚠️ Risks and Limitations


| Risk | Level | Mitigation |
|------|-------|------------|
| Inconsistent KPI definition | Medium | Prompt explicitly requires setting a single primary KPI in Section 1. |
| Scope creep in deliverables list | High | Inputs limited strictly to contracted target channels. |
| Missing legal disclaimers/CTAs | LOW | Added dedicated section for Mandatory Disclaimers/CTAs in output format. |

**Overall risk rating:** MEDIUM — Strategic misalignments can impact creative output, requiring mandatory strategist sign-off.

## 🔄 Version History

### v1.0 — Initial draft
**Date:** 2 August 2026
**Prompt:** Write a creative brief using these notes: [NOTES]
**Output:** Generic creative essay without structured sections or specific channel breakdowns.  
**Observed effect:** Designers could not use the output without extensive manual re-writing. 
**Lesson learned:** Needs structural section constraints and role definition.

---

### v1.1 — RACE Framework Integration
**Date:** 5 August 2026

**Output:** Much better layout, but missed linking target channels to specific deliverable formats.
**Observed effect:** Account manager had to manually map channels to ad formats.
**Lesson learned:** Must specify channel matrix in required output format.

---

### v1.2 — Multi-Input Chaining Refinement ✅ Current
**Date:** 7 August 2026 
**Change:** Updated prompt to explicitly chain P01 output alongside discovery call notes. Added word count limit.
**Output:** Concise, highly actionable creative brief aligned with brand voice and call takeaways.
**Observed effect:** Production time for briefs reduced to under 15 minutes.
**Lesson learned:** Combining structured upstream AI outputs with raw transcripts yields optimal strategy briefs.
